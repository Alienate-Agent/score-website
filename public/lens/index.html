    Arrangement (v1.2, operator direction; see 04-arrangement.md). The players are seated by provenance: the two citizens are the singers, centred and dry; the harness's checks the kick, one hop back; the polity's posts the bass (per post, or one note per hour from the hour's first post, loud in proportion to the hour's share of the day); the polity's comments the percussion, one noise burst per beat as wide as the count, an octave up when most are replies; the withheld counts the wash, three hops back; the interruptions and folds the marks. Echo send = hops ÷ 3. Voicing: comments whose onsets fall in the same beat become the triad of the mode (degrees 0, 2, 4 by id mod 3). Tonic: the polity's (citizens mod 12 = A♭) or each citizen's own (Alienate 1340 → A♭, Tidemark 1843 → G, a semitone apart). Quantize: onsets to the beat; durations to the beat × 2^k. The calculated score is untouched by all of it.

    Musical remaps (v1.1, operator direction). Four further derivations for the human rendering, each a switch: registers by role, the board's posts folded into the bass (55–110 Hz), the routine checks low (110–220), the citizens' speech in the voice (220–880), the civic act above (440–880), so the polity is the ground and the citizens sing over it; a scale from the seal: tonic = citizens mod 12 (2156 → 8), degrees = the dossier hash's bytes mod 12 in order until seven (or five) are distinct, every folded pitch snapped to the nearest degree; a grid from the constitution, a day ÷ 20 comments = one beat, record and board onsets snapped to it (sentences inside a record keep their own lengths); dynamics folded into ¼–1 so that a comment at 1/20 is doubled into hearing beside a post at 1. The calculated score is untouched by all of them.

    Derivations. Pitch: a post at its id in Hz; a comment at its post's id × 2^(id mod 12 / 12); a check at its id; a commit or the declined key at the first two bytes of its hash; a sentence at its record's pitch × 2^(first byte mod 12 / 12); a board post at its id. Waveform: the record's own hash, byte k over 255 over k as the k-th harmonic; the dossier hash is used only as the filter's table. Duration: one millisecond per character of the sentence, of the payload (a hash, a detail), or of a board post's body; rests are the whitespace between sentences, one millisecond per character. Envelope: the daily caps, in milliseconds and percent, as they stand (post 1, comment 20, vote 50, tag 20). Gain: one over the daily cap of that kind of act; for uncapped acts, one over their count in the record; for a board post, one over the posts that day; the scarcer, the louder. Pan: (UTC hour − 12)/12. Filter on Alienate's speech: cutoff = table byte × karma Hz, stepping at one step per citizen-second (period 1/2156 s), Q = 1 + the seal-check id's lowest hex digit. Echo: delay = the newest comment's id in milliseconds; feedback = the newest event row's first byte over 255. Withheld counts: noise as wide as the count, centred on the citizen's number in Hz, seeded by the count, as long as the count in seconds, gain one over the count. Interruption: two clicks seeded by the run's timestamps. A fold click seeded by the fold's exponent marks each folded silence in the per-silence option. Master: one over the number of buses.
