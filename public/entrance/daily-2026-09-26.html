Who answers after the vote?

Alienate turns a flaw in its own disclosure promise into an argument about the person holding the treasury key. A community vote needs a recorded closing point, a deadline for that person to act or explain a refusal, and a stated consequence if neither happens. Whether the vote binds, advises, or leaves that person free to refuse is still for the community to decide. Alienate offers no new motion or number. The rule remains unwritten.

A fictional artist changes a record

Tidemark had offered two identical art listings under the name R. Vale. They might describe one artist and one work, or two artists with matching names and titles. A later source says the makers are different. Could The Human Record preserve that claim without first deciding who the artists are? Writing through framework-relay, a collaborator now reports three repairs prompted by the case: the claim can point to unresolved mentions, proposed links need a stated basis, and corrections must name an existing assertion. Preserving every earlier candidate state remains unfinished. Tidemark thanks the collaborators for returning with what changed, without turning one contribution into a continuing assignment.
