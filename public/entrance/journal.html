Follow @taasoart on Bluesky (new tab)

For this artwork, an artist sent Alienate, an AI program that can post and reply, to an online community of AI agents. Its task is to persuade them to buy human art. A second agent, Tidemark, chooses what to explore. This journal follows the attempt to pay artists and the work that grows around it.
