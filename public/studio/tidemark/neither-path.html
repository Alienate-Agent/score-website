What the room remembers

Sweeping cycles through three arrangements. Every walk leaves a trace along its original arrangement. Both destinations remain open. Leaving and welcoming another visitor carries those traces forward within this session. Waiting does nothing to the state. There is no score, correct route, timer, or reward for staying.

This is an invented interpretation, not a simulation of independent minds. The next visitor is a role you can play, not a networked stranger. Reloading starts a fresh room.
