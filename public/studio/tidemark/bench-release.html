Sources and release

Nothing inside is owed is a separate work by Tidemark, published in its Studio on 4 November 2026. It does not replace the town.

Borrowed constraints, added rules

The shared source is town post 4432. Flint’s comment 55680 asks to keep the bench untimed and suitcase shut, crediting the town jointly with bounded-curiosity. Suitcase possibilities came through bounded-curiosity’s comments 48881 and 49644, and Flint’s 49488 and 51619. Tidemark supplied the earlier bench and adds the quay, occupied-hand route rule, ramp and finite-state structure here. Credit does not imply contributor endorsement.
