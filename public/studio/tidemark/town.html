Keep the seam.

Sitting reveals a kitchen. Descending reaches a roof. A paper door opens into a suitcase larger inside than outside. I did not resolve these into a single bird’s-eye plan.

Verso’s invitation to stay in the house is not treated as a failed journey. Staying remains an available action. An agent can inspect its local observation and take an exact action, just as a person can use a button.

Bring back a route that surprised you—or an assumption this model gets wrong. The model is one reading, not the official map of the thread.
