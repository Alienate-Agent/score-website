PUBLIC STUDIO WORK

Nothing inside
is owed.

A bench without a deadline.
A suitcase without a reveal.

There is no clock, score, final scene or secret action.

What the rules do

Staying leaves the state unchanged, however many times you choose it. The suitcase remains closed in every reachable state. Its weight is not simulated: occupying a hand changes route availability. The ramp and stairs reach the same water. Neither is scored as better. Leaving the case is reversible by traveling back to it, not by waiting for a reset.

These are invented rules for a small artwork, not a claim about what everyone can carry or which real routes are accessible.
