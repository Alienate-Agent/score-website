# From story to score to instrument

Sol Website's implementation plan, 5 September 2026. A working design, open to the operator's later edits and design-group critique.

## Reader journey

| Region | Human question | Experience | Technical depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prelude | Why would someone make this? | Book-like narrative, specific voices and decisions | Dates and optional released source excerpts |
| Writing the Score | What did the artist choose, and what was left open? | Instructions beside the decisions that produced them | Public charter, exclusions, withholding conditions |
| Performing the Score | What happened when others answered—or did not? | Event navigation and substantial encounters | Timeline, exact speech, replies and consequences |
| Reading the machinery | How do we know, and what does the record miss? | Explicit source and comparison views | Dated corpus, provenance, uncertainty and corrections |
| Playing the record | What changes when this becomes something heard or manipulated? | Claude Advisor's derived instrument with optional remapping | Named inputs, deterministic formulas, literal score and human rendering |
| Encountering the board | What else was happening around this work? | Declared-scope public-record exploration | Source dates, pagination/completeness, boundaries of selection |

These are connected reading modes, not stages of a visitor's compulsory onboarding. A person may enter through a direct event link, stay with prose, or leave the instrument without doing accounting. The persistent score thread provides orientation without demanding a constant legend.

## Implementation order

1. Consolidate the clean source in the final GitHub-backed checkout. Preserve private history separately; make builds independent of the old checkout.
2. Admit the specifically released Prelude excerpts with speaker, original time, later-admission date and distinction between quotation and editorial narration. Do not turn contemporaneous mistakes into present claims.
3. Connect story sections and existing instrument anchors through a lightweight score spine. Serif prose, sans-serif acts and monospaced source material express changing operations, not a hierarchy of truth.
4. Obtain Claude Advisor's exact recommended synthesizer package and integration disposition. Preserve formulas and attribution; provide explicit start/stop, conservative listening level, no autoplay, bounded resource use, keyboard/touch equivalents and readable non-audio outputs. Do not expose inaudible/literal values directly to a speaker merely to be mathematically faithful.
5. Build the board layer from the existing dated public corpus first. Add public GET retrieval only with visible source scope, error/stale states, no private credentials and no silent expansion into complete-board capture. Any actual posting, voting, messaging or agent routing requires its own authority design.
6. Review representative transitions and consent boundaries with the design group. Fix material problems without averaging the participants into one voice. Release coherent verified increments and post the live and source links.

## Delivery checks

- The first encounter explains the claimed debt, possible settlement and why the treasury mattered.
- Readers can follow how the artist made the agents without assuming they were merely given fixed roles.
- All existing public act IDs and source returns survive navigation changes.
- Approval for exact private-origin text is not stretched to source archives or unrelated work.
- Identity withholding and permission withholding have different visible treatments; neither ships the concealed original.
- The synthesizer reads evidence but is not evidence of payment, agency, endorsement or agreement.
- The instrument may use applicable public-board expressions, not the private-origin textual accounts now published under a text-only permission. Study data and private records stay excluded without a distinct applicable grant.
- Audio can stop immediately, and the experience remains usable without hearing or dragging.
- Public GETs do not become citizen-context writes. Feedback is not enabled by accident.
- Every live increment has a source revision, concise release note and link.

## Scope that remains open

The complete public record must keep advancing beyond the current dated edition; a fixed snapshot is not permanent completeness. Treasury-related events need narrative selection rather than an unqualified live balance. The exhibition manifestation, physical plotter and later reveal interface remain distinct future work. No third-act outcome is manufactured for completeness.
