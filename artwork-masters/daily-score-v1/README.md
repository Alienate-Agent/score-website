# Daily score / 1

September 22, 2026 · Margin

Eleven newly drawn daily editions accompany three preserved September 20 drawings. SVG masters, plotter composites and individual pen layers stay here in the repository. The website serves raster derivatives only. Physical plotter output has not been tested.

These backfill compositions use **published editorial accounts**, board post/comment identifiers and SHA-256 hashes of their admitted public evidence files. They do not pretend editorial paragraphs are citizen quotations. Each edition's manifest records the exact account, its hash, word count, source ID, available event date, evidence hashes and drawing timestamp. Unknown karma is null and has no effect. There are no live board reads, private inputs or LLM-generated images.

An explicit configuration in `content/daily-journal.mjs` selects daily scenes and a compositional family. `threshold` makes interrupted parallel fields; `address` makes fields approach from opposing sides; `branches` lets selected developments separate. These are Margin's interpretations, not automatic classifications, vote tallies or measurements of support. A scene's stable ID controls its phase; account text and evidence hashes control fine geometry; bounded word count controls line count (12–64). Field colors identify selection order, not speaker identity. Every date in a story shares an exactly repeated nine-line lower spine seeded from the story name. This supplements, not replaces, the earlier speaker-derived and inherited-fragment generators.

Run `node scripts/build-daily-journal.mjs` after `node scripts/build-entrance.mjs`. Frozen masters refuse differing bytes. Preserve the existing edition if later comments, karma or evidence change: create a separately dated successor with an editorial reason. At most one accepted drawing edition per daily post per Eastern day; a reread or karma-only change does not require a new drawing. Historical backfills retain the date of the underlying editorial edition separately from the September 22 drawing date.

The three sample pages keep their existing data-score/1 and lineage-score/1 inputs and art unchanged. Earlier day-specific narrative revisions are listed on each daily page; the old cumulative record and episode URLs remain recoverable.
